HOW THE BOOKING ENGINE WORKS

12 business
rules, in plain language.

These summarize the rules in the supplied PS-108 design document and the current demo behavior. Some financial details still need organizer confirmation.

Cancellation refund noticeRefund eligibility is calculated as amounts paid minus capped forfeiture, never below zero. Any amount due above what was paid is shown separately, up to the BR-07 cap. This demo does not transfer money.
BR-01

Holds and queues

An available date and slot can be held for 72 hours. Expired holds release the slot; queued enquiries are handled in order. Confirmation requires the matching active hold, and the first advance clears the overlapping queue.

BR-02

Venue pricing

The demo uses the higher applicable seasonal multiplier: 1.3× for weekends and 1.6× for auspicious dates. A day-and-night booking gets 10% off the second slot.

BR-03

Catering

The minimum billed quantity is the higher of 80% of quoted guests (rounded up) or 150 plates. Plates up to 10% above the quote use the standard rate; additional plates use 1.2×.

BR-04

Package swaps

Swaps are free when 30 or more days remain. Inside 30 days, the demo adds a fee of 10% of the higher old or new package price.

BR-05

Advance schedule

More than 60 days out: 25% at confirmation, 50% at 60 days, and the balance at 7 days. At 8–60 days: 75% at confirmation and 25% at 7 days. At 7 days or fewer: full payment at confirmation.

BR-06

Late payments

The implementation applies a 5-day grace period, then 1% for each started overdue week. An outstanding advance at 21 days overdue triggers automatic cancellation in the demo.

BR-07

Cancellation ladder

General forfeiture is 10% more than 180 days out, 25% at 91–180 days, 50% at 31–90, 75% at 8–30, and 100% within 7 days. The total cap is advances paid plus ₹25,000.

BR-08

Vendor cancellation terms

Photography forfeiture is 100% within 30 days. Catering forfeiture within 7 days is based on the catering guarantee. The general cap still applies to the combined amount.

BR-09

Date changes

One free date change is allowed when the original event is more than 90 days away and the replacement date is within 180 days of the request. Otherwise, the demo calculates cancellation forfeiture and a 50% rebooking credit, which must be applied manually to a separate booking.

BR-10

Final settlement

The bill brings together venue, catering actuals, packages, switch fees, rate-card damages, GST, and advances; tips are excluded. GST is 18% on venue and packages, and 5% on catering without input tax credit. Damages are outside those stated GST bases.

BR-11

Commissions and payouts

Configured commissions are 12% for venues, 8% for catering, and 15% for décor and photography. Payouts become eligible 7 days after event close; a matching open dispute filed within 48 hours freezes the payout.

BR-12

Rule precedence

Vendor-specific cancellation terms are calculated first, the general ladder applies to remaining contract value, then the ₹25,000 cap is applied once to the combined forfeiture.

This is a local demo: booking holds, enquiry queues and booking states persist in a local SQLite database. Payments are simulated, and no cash refund is processed. Ask the organizer to confirm unresolved policies before treating estimates as final terms.
