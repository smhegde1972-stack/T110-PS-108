MASTER DATA

Register venues and vendors

10. Register venue

Season pricing uses the rule engine: weekday 1.0×, weekend 1.3×, auspicious date 1.6× (maximum applicable multiplier, not stacked). Day + night receives 10% off the second slot.

11. Register vendor

Commission is fixed by the design rule: catering 8%; decor and photography 15%. Catering guarantee follows BR-03: the higher of 80% of quoted guests or 150 plates.

Registration currently changes the running Java process only. To retain new master data after restart, update the CSV files or add persistent storage.
